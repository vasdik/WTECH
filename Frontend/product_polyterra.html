Description
Reduced plastic content

PolyTerra PLA is a bioplastic filament for 3D printing in which organic materials are combined with PLA to reduce the plastic content and create a more environmentally friendly filament.

Easy processing

PolyTerra prints just like PLA, so there is no need to change any print settings, and its overhang and bridging capabilities can even surpass those of PLA. With a wide printing temperature range of 190 to 230 °C, PolyTerra products can be processed on any extrusion-based 3D printer.

Numerous possibilities for use

The mechanical properties of PolyTerra offer a wide range of possible applications, as the material is stronger than conventional PLA while retaining good rigidity. This allows for the printing of figures and accessories, as the additional strength built into PolyTerra does not affect the functionality of simple printing.
